Uptick · Privacy Policy

Your progress. Your device.

Game saves

Uptick saves your game in app-owned files on this iPhone. There is no game account, cloud sync or server. Deleting the app removes its saves, so export a backup first if you want to keep your progress.

Backups

Export creates a JSON file containing your game progress. You choose where to save it and whether to share it. Restoring replaces current progress only after you review and confirm it.

No tracking

Uptick does not include advertising, third-party analytics or tracking, and it collects no personal data. The game does not send your progress, balances or activity anywhere.

Purchases

Uptick Premium is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes your payment; Uptick only receives Apple's verified confirmation that Premium is owned on your Apple ID.

Reminders

Reminders are off unless you turn them on in Game settings. When on, Uptick schedules notifications on this iPhone through iOS (for example when offline earnings are full or a daily dividend is ready). Nothing is sent to a server. Turn them off in Game settings or the iPhone Settings app.

Bug reports

The support report contains the app version, device model, iOS version and save status. It excludes balances, holdings, backups and purchase receipts. Copying a report does not send it anywhere; you choose what to share.

Children

Uptick does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no personal information at all.

Changes

If Uptick ever adds a feature that handles data differently, this policy will be updated before that version is released. Last updated October 2026.
